Medbridge Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Medbridge ("the app") is published by ANT Youth Foundation. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

What Stays on Your Device

Your notes, session transcripts and history are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive them, and we do not create accounts or ask for your name, email or phone number.

Information Sent Off Your Device

To work, the app sends limited information to outside services:

  • Medication searches: the medication names you search for are sent to public medical databases, including RxNorm (U.S. National Library of Medicine), openFDA and MedlinePlus, to look up information.
  • Translation: text from your transcripts or medication pages may be sent to a third-party translation service so it can be translated into Chinese.
  • Voice recognition: when you use voice recognition, your speech is processed by Apple's speech recognition service. Apple's handling of that audio is described in Apple's privacy policy.

We use this information only to make the app work. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to track you across other apps and websites. These services have their own privacy policies and we do not control how they handle data.

Permissions

The app asks for access to your microphone and speech recognition so it can turn your voice into a transcript and recognize medication names. You can turn these off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Keeping and Deleting Your Data

You can delete any transcript or note inside the app, and deleting the app removes all data stored on your device.

Children

Medbridge is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Medical Disclaimer

Medbridge is for education only. It does not diagnose, treat or replace advice from a doctor or pharmacist.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.

Contact
